Attribution Statement

The Adventurers pack holds nine rigged, animated characters by Kay Lousberg (KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0 and Skeletons 1.0, kaylousberg.com), rebuilt for theprototype: weapons removed, headgear baked into the skin, every part merged into one mesh on one material, the gradient atlas reduced to 256², and ten of the clips kept (tools/avatars/build.mjs). The same characters are the app's player avatars.

Kay Lousberg dedicated the source packs to the public domain under CC0 1.0, and this pack keeps that license: use it for anything, no credit required (crediting Kay Lousberg is appreciated by the author).
