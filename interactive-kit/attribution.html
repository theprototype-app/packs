Attribution Statement

The Interactive Kit was made by theprototype. Most of its pieces are the Modular Architecture, Props & Interiors and Sci-fi kits' own models, split into a static frame and a hinged moving part and animated with the packs repo's embedded tools (tools/anim). Six meshes (the studded door leaf, the iron gate, the portcullis, the wardrobe, the chest of drawers and the ceiling fan) were made with Meshy.ai (text-to-3D on a paid plan, whose Customer Output belongs to the customer) and post-processed here.

It is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: use it for anything, no credit required (a mention of theprototype is always welcome).
