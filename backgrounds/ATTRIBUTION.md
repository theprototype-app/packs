- **Fluid set** (`mesh-gradient`, `silk-gradient`, `noise-flow`, `metaballs`, `grain-dither`) — © theprototype,

- **Gradient family** (`ember-ribbon`, `violet-orb`, `mint-tide`, `glass-orbit`, `night-swell`, `indigo-bloom`,
