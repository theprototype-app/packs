Attribution Statement

The Characters pack holds humanoid characters for theprototype's Characters & motion tools: every one maps onto VRM 1.0's humanoid bones, so any motion-kit clip (or any other humanoid clip) plays on it.

  • VRM avatars (Astronaut, Chill Penguin, Cool Pirate, Erika, Kate, Olivia, Skelly, Witch) from 100Avatars R1 / R2 by Polygonal Mind, listed by the Open Source Avatars registry. Each file is shipped as its authors made it and states its licence in its own VRM meta: CC0, commercial use allowed.
  • Mannequins (female, male) by Quaternius, from the Universal Animation Library 2 / 1 Standard (quaternius.com, OpenGameArt). The male mannequin's clips were taken out (they ship once, in motion-kit).

All of it is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: use it for anything, no credit required (crediting Polygonal Mind and Quaternius is appreciated by the authors). licences.json records the licence, author and source of every file; the pack CI's licence lint checks each VRM's embedded licence against it.
