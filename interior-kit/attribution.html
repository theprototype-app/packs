Made with Meshy.ai: the sculpted pieces (round and long tables, bench, bar stool and stool, bar counter, back-bar shelves, crate of fruit, wingback armchair, sofa, double bed, wardrobe, snake plant, writing desk, kitchen counter, range stove, stone fireplace) were generated with Meshy's text-to-3D API on a paid plan (Meshy Terms of Use §3.2: paid-plan customers own their output), then post-processed by theprototype's tools/meshy pipeline and interior-kit/_src/build.mjs — re-meshed to a game budget, scaled to real metres, re-pivoted, broken normals and unpainted texels repaired, colours graded to the kit's palette, textures resized. The wall trims (skirting, wainscot, cornice), the lights (wall sconce, floor lamp, chandelier), the round rug, the framed picture and the shelf of books were built procedurally by theprototype (interior-kit/_src/procedural.mjs); the two furniture sets are arranged from the finished pieces.

Meshy's own generator metadata is kept inside each GLB (Meshy ToS §2.4).
