Made with Meshy.ai: the sculpted pieces (furniture, barrels, crates, chest, sacks, lantern, candles, plant, cauldron, well, market stall, sci-fi console and crate) were generated with Meshy's text-to-3D API on a paid plan (Meshy Terms of Use §3.2: paid-plan customers own their output), then post-processed by theprototype's tools/meshy pipeline — re-meshed to a game budget, scaled to real metres, re-pivoted, textures resized. The game-logic and flat pieces (lever, wall button, pressure plate, trapdoor, key, ladder, rug, tapestry, signpost, wall torch) and every flame were built procedurally by theprototype (props-kit/_src/procedural.mjs).

Meshy's own generator metadata is kept inside each GLB (Meshy ToS §2.4).
