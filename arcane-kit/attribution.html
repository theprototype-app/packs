Made with Meshy.ai: every piece (crystal ball, alchemist's table, lectern with spellbook, telescope, armillary sphere, potion shelf, rune rug) was generated with Meshy's text-to-3D API on a paid plan (Meshy Terms of Use §3.2: paid-plan customers own their output), then post-processed by theprototype's tools/meshy pipeline — re-meshed to a game budget, scaled to real metres, re-pivoted, textures resized — and given levels of detail and a z-fight pass by tools/lod. The rug was laid flat (2 cm) by hand.

Meshy's own generator metadata is kept inside each GLB (Meshy ToS §2.4).
