Attribution Statement

The Motion pack holds 87 humanoid animation clips by Quaternius: the free Standard tiers of the Universal Animation Library 1 and 2 (quaternius.com, UAL 1, UAL 2) — locomotion, jumps, sitting, swimming, combat, interactions and more.

Each clip is its own .tpanim file: a skeleton plus one animation, valid VRM Animation inside (rename one to .vrma and VRM tools play it). theprototype retargets a clip onto any mapped humanoid — the characters-kit avatars, the player avatars, a Mixamo, Meshy or UniRig rig.

Quaternius dedicated the libraries to the public domain under CC0 1.0, and this pack keeps that license: use it for anything, no credit required (crediting Quaternius is appreciated by the author).
