Nature & Terrain is a kit of stylised-realistic outdoor assets for building levels in theprototype.app: trees, bushes, grass and ferns, flowers, rocks, a modular cliff wall, a stone path tile, logs, a pond, a footbridge, a walkable mound and a fallen-leaves decal.

© theprototype, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: use it for anything, no credit required.


The models were made with Meshy.ai (text-to-3D on a paid plan, whose output belongs to the plan holder) and post-processed by theprototype (scale, pivot, simplification, texture size and colour grading). Grass tufts, the leaves decal and the pond water are procedural. The Meshy generation metadata in each file is kept as it came.
